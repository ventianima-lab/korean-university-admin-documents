# Procurement forms and evidence pages

Read this reference before proposing a programme budget, including a chat estimate, or drafting or revising purchase preapproval, purchase inspection records, expense settlement, vendor quotations, receipt attachment forms, or evidence-photo pages. Use the user's current edited files as the baseline. Keep active records in their working folder when the user requests that; organization is not permission to archive them.

## Permission and factual fields

- A request to prepare a document includes the user's clearance with the named vendor or party for that preparation and requested use of supplied names, seals, and signatures. Do not ask whether that permission exists or request it again.
- Treat dates, amounts, and party instructions supplied by the user as source facts. Distinguish missing business data from permission; inspect context and files before asking only for a value necessary to finish. Do not infer missing approval states or create an absent signature image.
- Use an existing supplied stamp or signature image when requested. Preserve its identity and proportions; do not draw, generate, or substitute a new seal. Preparation does not authorize sending or submission.


### Original issuer master and non-text identity inventory

Before reconstructing quotation metadata, select the actual supplied issuer master, not an agent-generated substitute, and inventory its non-text issuer identity elements: embedded/linked images (including GIF), seals, signatures, logos, their source object/stream locations, hashes when available, placement and issuer association. Inspect the original HWP's OLE/BinData or an identity-preserving conversion when needed; text extraction, a blank generated draft, or absent OCR seal text cannot establish that the source seal is absent. Presence of an image and semantic recognition as the issuer's seal are separate findings: retain uncertain images and record recognition as unresolved rather than calling them absent.

Use that original form as the writable-copy baseline and preserve its authentic identity objects and proportions while changing only authorized fields. Do not recreate a generic quotation from extracted metadata when the supplied master exists. Existing party clearance covers the requested preparation; do not re-ask it. Never copy another issuer's seal, draw a replacement, or imply official reissue/current confirmation from a revised copy. An older identity certificate can support a source-owner match, not current registration status; do not make certificates a new requirement for every lecturer.

When post-save checking is authorized, extend the existing template-input invocation with `identity_assets`: one independently inventoried `{source_member, target_member, sha256, role, recognition}` entry for each preserved source identity asset, where recognition is `recognized` or `unresolved`. For converted HWP, bind the conversion to the original object's inventory independently. `verify_input_coverage.py --template-inventory` compares those package bytes; it does not recognize seals, discover missing inventory entries, prove issuer identity or verify placement/rendering. Its text-only PASS cannot establish seal preservation or full readiness. A missing source seal in the candidate must fail the asset-preservation check. Under an explicit no-post-edit-review instruction, perform only pre-edit source inventory and planned preservation; do not invoke this or any actual-output check, and disclose verification as waived.

### Explicit roster role order and whole-person movement

Derive role order from the user's instruction for that event and form; do not invent a universal personnel hierarchy. For example, an explicitly instructed `학과장 → 강사 → 조교 → 학생` sequence applies only to its stated scope. Reuse the current supplied roster/sign-in form and update its existing full records; do not create a duplicate generic roster. Keep event and insurance populations independent even when their totals differ by one.

Before editing, freeze each full source-backed person record and its role, name, student/person ID, contact details and other supplied fields, including meaningful blanks. Move the entire record together, with its row-specific associations; swapping only names leaves another person's ID/contact behind. Preserve source order within each role unless directed otherwise and leave authentic signature evidence untouched.

When checking is authorized, invoke `check_person_record_order(source_records, output_records, role_order, key_field)` in the existing `verify_roster_fields.py` for each affected population, in addition to the existing exact saved-cell comparison. Independently transcribe output records from the actual rows; provide the user's ordered roles and an independently sourced stable key field. Compare all record fields, not name sets or totals. This helper checks provided complete records and order, not source authority or completeness of their transcription. Under no-post-edit-review, use the frozen source records only to plan the row movement and waive all actual saved-row checks; synthetic regression tests do not establish actual-document readiness.

## Requested quotation revisions

For explicitly authorized vendor-form date or usage corrections, bind each requested value to its exact writable field and latest user instruction. A directed entry in an originally blank author-fillable date field does not authorize filling other blank dates, altering genuine signer/approval controls, or treating the date as evidence of issue, execution or attendance. Correct only the identified usage/date associations and verify every targeted occurrence against that independent map, preserving existing author entries and unrelated source evidence. Preserve issuer originals separately; when the user requests clean active filenames without a revision suffix, keep provenance and revision status outside the filename rather than adding an unwanted tag. A clean name or reused authentic signature does not establish official reissue, renewed signing, vendor confirmation or acceptance.

Before preparing or revising a vendor quotation or related purchase documents, inspect the current supplier registration certificate when available, match it to the actual supplier, and compare its stated registration status and any confirmed tax wording with the quotation and other current copies. Record a concrete conflict separately and seek issuer confirmation; do not infer the tax treatment of every supply from an exempt registration alone. When the user explicitly corrects the requested wording, reflect the exact instruction in the authorized draft, clearly distinguish the proposal from an issuer-confirmed quotation, and preserve unsupported tax conclusions as unresolved. Before handoff, check the exact saved wording and tax amounts across all current in-scope copies, record any conflict or pending issuer confirmation, and do not claim issuer confirmation without evidence. Do not edit unrelated records or replace a user-edited final to force consistency.


When asked to change amounts or tax wording on an issuer's quotation, preserve the latest original and distinguish the user's requested revision as a draft or confirmation proposal; do not present the altered document as a quotation issued or confirmed by the vendor. Keep explicit user instructions separate from facts unverified in the original, including whether the issuer has accepted the changes.

For each directed row, independently calculate unit price from the specified row amount divided by the specified quantity. Check the quantity and multiplication back to the row amount, then independently sum all applicable rows and reconcile the displayed total and any explicitly supported tax, discount or delivery conditions. If division or rounding prevents an exact reconciliation, preserve the instructed amounts and report the unresolved discrepancy instead of inventing a rounding rule or changing a value silently.

Before reporting the revision complete, reopen the exact saved output and compare each directed row, quantity, unit price and row amount with an independently prepared instruction-to-row map. Verify the requested VAT wording, such as `부가세 포함`, is actually present in the correct saved location and visible in its current rendering. Independently recalculate the complete total from the saved row amounts and supported conditions; check the original is preserved and the proposal is clearly distinguishable from issuer confirmation. Missing text, a mismatched calculation, an unverified issuer assertion or an unresolved rounding discrepancy blocks a completion claim for that part. A successful save or an input-only calculation does not pass this check.

Keep unsupported instructions unresolved only where affected; do not invent missing facts or substitute a generic manual requirement.
- Distinguish issuer-owned certificates and quotations to obtain or verify from received forms the user must fill. Use the actual received master and caller-specific field instructions; a generic blank contract does not satisfy a request to complete a supplied form. Record a missing or conflicting required version as pending. User authorization to prepare a document does not establish issuer confirmation, signatures, submission or acceptance.
- For each request ID, record its disposition and evidence, actual saved output path/hash, exact output field or action location, independently sourced expected value, observed saved value and verification status. Bind these field expectations to the source-backed input completeness check below; run its existing template-inventory mode where the form fits, otherwise perform an equivalent independent source-to-field comparison, including image-only inputs. Re-read every requested field in the exact saved output and verify the required version and master identity. Do not build expectations from the candidate itself.
- Before claiming a requested packet complete, explicitly list the request IDs and recipient scope covered by that claim, and reconcile every in-scope request against this inventory and the actual saved forms, completed inputs or separately evidenced actions. Use `scripts/verify_required_packet.py <private-inventory.json>` for its supported requirement/artifact readiness checks, mapping request IDs into its existing schema; retain the independent communication-to-request and field checks because the helper cannot discover omitted speech instructions or verify this inventory's extra fields. A matching filename, complete school attachment list, curated form allowlist or passing selected-field test alone does not establish vendor-request coverage. Preparation is distinct from sending or submission.

## Source-backed input completeness

When copying contact information, independently map the owner and contact function (telephone/TEL, fax/FAX, mobile or other labeled field) from the original source label to the target field before entering the value. A number's presence in the right company's record does not establish that it is a telephone number; do not transfer a nearby fax value into a telephone field. Include the source path and exact labeled location, target coordinates and independently read expected value in the field inventory, then run the existing `scripts/validate_hwpx_opensource.py <saved.hwpx> --fields <inventory.json> --fields-only` or an equivalent saved-field comparison. Compare the complete target label/value association, not just digits found elsewhere. Keep conflicting or ambiguously labeled contacts pending for source/office confirmation rather than inventing a replacement.

Approval/cooperation role labels and example routes printed in a form or earlier document do not prove the current programme's mandatory approvers. Before treating them as confirmed input, independently verify their current relevance and applicability from the governing instructions or responsible office; copying a previous footer is not that check. Preserve fixed form labels. If the user expressly authorizes proposed routing roles under an active red-marking instruction, mark only the proposed unconfirmed role characters in scope, leave unsigned signature fields blank, and make no approval-complete claim. Do not automatically recolor or delete all routing labels. Report an unresolved mandatory route for office confirmation outside the document rather than inventing it.

For the requested form, identify the official template input areas and independently enumerate each applicable input before looking at the completed candidate. Map the exact template label and input coordinates to the saved output label and input coordinates, its disposition, exact expected content and independent evidence. Check the latest user instruction, a finalized plan for the same year and management code, and available source documents before leaving a business field unconfirmed. A source-supported goal is a planned target, not a completed outcome. Preserve user-entered values and distinguish controlled approval, signature and consent fields, unconfirmed figures and actual results; do not invent or clear them. Do not call the document complete because the few fields selected by the author happen to pass.

For a text-table region, run `python scripts/verify_input_coverage.py <saved.hwpx> <private-inventory.json> --template-inventory`. The private JSON object contains `template` with `path`, `sha256`, `section`, `table`; a `scope` array of independently identified `{row_start, row_end, input_cols}` source-form regions; and `fields` entries. Each field has `source: {row, col, label_col, label}` and `target: {section, table, row, col, label_col, label}`; set `label_row` when the label is in a different row. `status` is `known`, `preserved`, `controlled`, `unconfirmed`, or `unused` with a specific `reason`. A `known` or `preserved` field also requires exact `expected`, `value_kind` (`plan`, `fact`, `actual`, or `user_preserved`), and independent `evidence`: each document item has `kind: document`, `path`, `sha256`, `location`; a user instruction has `kind: user_instruction`, `reference`, `quote`. An unconfirmed blank requires two distinct checked `lookups`. Run the existing default mode for dispositions of other saved blank cells when applicable.

For each selected table with fixed total labels, run `python scripts/verify_input_coverage.py <saved.hwpx> <private-total-inventory.json> --total-labels`. The inventory has `source` with frozen template `path`, `sha256`, `section`, zero-based `table`; `output` with saved `section` and `table`; and `mappings`, one `{source: [row, col], target: [row, col]}` per total-label cell using zero-based HWPX cell addresses. The helper discovers every source cell whose complete text, ignoring whitespace, is `계`, `합계`, or `총계`, requires unique mappings, and compares each mapped label and row/column span. An omitted occurrence fails even if the same label survives in another block. Independently identify all applicable source tables and verify each mapping's business context, amount, and rendered row; the helper cannot establish authority, other fixed labels, values, formatting, or visual layout. Complex nested-table or image cells require separate review. An explicitly authorized structural change requires a separately documented source-to-output review, not a false passing mapping.

The checker enumerates the source cells within the recorded scope, refuses omitted or duplicate source/target mappings, checks both labels, exact saved text, source/evidence hashes and controlled blanks, and rejects the output as its own evidence. It cannot establish that the selected scope covers the entire form, authenticate user instructions, interpret source evidence, compare image-only inputs or establish consent and approval. Independently review the full form, unsupported/non-text areas and evidence relationships; inspect the current render and apply preservation checks separately. A blank exact output where a supported planned value is expected must fail even if it is mislabeled unconfirmed; reconcile the disposition against the sources before running the check.

## Preapproval packet completeness

### Composite evidence role map before declaring absence

Before calling requested vendor/travel papers missing, inspect the complete relevant PDF/HWP/HWPX candidates, including later pages and embedded scans. Filename search, indexed previews and extraction limits locate candidates; they cannot establish role absence. Build the role map in the response for a read-only search, or in an already authorized preparation inventory: requested role -> exact current file, page and field/heading -> issuer/company, vehicle identifier where applicable, covered person/group or risk, and effective period versus the target trip. Record uninspected or unreadable pages as unresolved, never absent. Use format-level extraction and task-authorized page inspection/OCR for image-only regions; do not infer their content from surrounding text.

A combined file may supply several evidence aspects. Map each independently before invoking the existing packet gate, and allow one file to support multiple role entries only to the extent its inspected pages support them. A registration page's inspection dates or a validity endorsement establishes that field, not the existence of a separate inspection certificate or its acceptance as a substitute. Keep document presence, related field evidence, standalone certificate existence and receiving-office acceptance distinct. A prepared packet with unmapped evidence locations is not grounds for declaring the source papers absent.

Classify passenger/traveler insurance, vehicle liability or mutual-aid coverage, and a company's guarantee/bond from the actual issuer, insured subject, coverage and period. A generic insurance-certificate filename does not establish its kind, and one kind cannot silently satisfy another role. Search historical same-company packets within the authorized scope when requested; compare vehicle and validity independently. A different vehicle or expired period makes that candidate historical reference for the current trip, even when company identity matches. Report historical availability separately from current-trip availability; either can exist without proving the other. Do not infer a universal legal document requirement from these examples.

A read-only request authorizes inspection and a source-cited role map, not copying historical papers, rewriting a checklist, moving records or editing prepared documents. An earlier waiver of post-save QA does not bar a newly explicit read-only source inspection; it does not retroactively authorize saved-output QA. Preserve any currently reserved review scope. The completion check is a role-by-role account of inspected pages/fields, identity and period matches, unresolved kinds, and remaining standalone/acceptance questions; a packet checker only checks the supplied invocation and cannot establish these semantics itself.
