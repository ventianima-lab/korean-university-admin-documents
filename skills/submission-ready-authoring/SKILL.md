# Complete document authoring

When a user selects complete-for-submission preparation with red marking of unconfirmed assistant choices, honor that current request or standing preference across its applicable document-writing tasks without repeated permission questions. A later explicit task restriction controls; do not assume every user's opt-in.

For requested document artifacts, produce the complete usable document or packet within the authorized scope. Calling a working file a draft does not justify leaving writable fields or required attachments unfinished. Respect a deliberate outline-only, review-only or limited-field request; this policy does not authorize changing unrelated records.

## Fill from an independent inventory

Before asking how to split or combine programmes, packets or output folders, inspect the current business folders and their latest working documents and output sets. Map each requested programme to its established folder, packet boundary and destination. Preserve that grouping without duplicate confirmation when the current structure and contents establish it; naming alone, archived copies or a generic preference question are not substitutes for inspection. Ask only about a material ambiguity that remains after inspection or a genuine requested change, identifying the unresolved choice. Pass this source-backed grouping map to any authoring worker before it writes; do not merge, split, move or duplicate packets merely to impose a new organization.

Find and inspect the current official or user-designated form, instructions, current source records and applicable precedents. Use the latest working final for follow-ups. Before entry, independently enumerate all real inputs and required attachments, including repeated fields, and map exact source locations and output coordinates. Keep fixed labels, decorative blanks and signer-controlled areas outside ordinary fill scope. A list copied from the candidate cannot establish completeness. Enumerate by document and page or region, including metadata inputs on photo-evidence sheets as well as picture slots; for each repeated session map its date and location separately to the governing source. Confirm every authorized form and region was reviewed before a selected-scope check can support packet completion. Preserve decorative blanks, signature and approval areas, and distinguish planned dates from actual attendance.

When the authoritative form itself instructs preparers to remove a guidance or example section before submission, classify that instruction separately from fixed form content. For a submission-ready copy, remove the specified guidance from the saved document rather than merely hiding it in the print area or reporting it for later; preserve the source and unrelated fields, formatting, and print settings. Reopen the exact saved copy and render it to verify the guidance is absent and the required form remains intact. Do not remove guidance where the user directs its retention or the instruction does not apply.

For preapproval packets, first apply the phase-and-owner inventory and scoped checker invocation in [the procurement forms reference](../hancom-hwpx-documents/references/procurement-forms.md). A later office-owned contract after approval is not a missing current department attachment; assess separately requested unsigned review forms on their own stage and recipient. For accompanying faculty/staff travel, apply its separate pretrip-role check: a matching return report does not establish preparation of a required application or completion of an ERP approval action. Honor an explicit instruction to leave post-edit document review to the user and report the actual preparation status.

For itinerary revisions, apply the [source-to-schedule gate](../hancom-hwpx-documents/references/procurement-forms.md#itinerary-revision-source-to-schedule-gate-before-writing) before authoring: derive the retained activity/meal/transfer chain and map every authorized existing date/time field independently. Keep a schedule-only new stop out of goals, budgets and narrative while updating authorized linked times and preserving paid-session durations and fees. Apply gap closure only when requested; a post-save-review waiver skips actual-record QA, not this pre-write mapping.

For expense packets, apply the expense-type form and claim-activity reconciliation in [the procurement forms reference](../hancom-hwpx-documents/references/procurement-forms.md) before drafting and again against the saved packet. Programme identity, a combined amount, or a selected output list cannot establish which dedicated forms or activity-specific evidence are required.

For physical receipts, inventory the receipt evidence and any attachment form as separate roles: map who obtains and attaches the original, and who prepares the form, using the current instructions and applicable official template. A user's decision to attach originals personally or omit receipt images does not exclude an applicable or requested attachment form. Respect an explicit exclusion of the form; do not require one universally. Before handoff, reconcile the saved packet against this role inventory and visually verify any requested physical mounting space is present and empty. Do not fabricate or print a receipt in that space. A user-managed physical original does not by itself exclude separate supplied electronic receipts: classify each actual receipt by department, medium and intended handling, then apply the receipt assembly and saved-output gate in the procurement forms reference. Leave mounting space only for actual physical receipts assigned to the user.

For requested identity-card and bankbook-copy attachments, inventory the source images and the applicable attachment form as separate roles. Existing JPGs or other image assets alone do not satisfy preparation of a requested current form. Before writing, find and inspect the actual eligible current official or user-designated form and its instructions; map each authentic source image to its person, document role, intended slot and required person/group/page arrangement independently of file order. Use the form's prescribed grouping, labels and image placement, preserve source originals separately and unchanged, and fit authentic content without distortion or loss of required content, preserving image aspect ratio and the form's instructions. Do not substitute a generic page, invented heading, generated identity image, transcribed sensitive values or extra PDF. If the form or person-to-slot mapping is unavailable or ambiguous, report that specific preparation dependency without treating assets as a completed form. Applicability comes from the current user scope and forms, not a universal requirement for all programmes. Keep personal identifiers, account numbers and source photos out of chat and public audit/test artifacts.

For these identity attachments, keep source-before-write form and person/slot mapping even when the user explicitly waives post-save verification. Honor that waiver: do not reopen, render, hash, parse or otherwise check the actual saved output as QA; report preparation and the waived check accurately. An authentic source-bound form prepared in the requested arrangement supports a preparation report only, not verified identity, account ownership, consent, approval or submission. When verification is authorized, reconcile the saved form with the independent role/slot inventory under the normal completion gate.

Likewise distinguish user-controlled signing or attendance from preparation of an applicable or requested meeting sign-in form. Track each meeting's form, existing signed original, preparation responsibility, and inclusion or exclusion separately; a later explicit inclusion supersedes an earlier exclusion for its stated scope only. Do not require a new sign-in form for every event. Prefer the existing authentic signed original when applicable and preserve it unchanged; use authorized identity sources for any prepared form, leave unsupported signatures blank, and never infer attendance from a printed name. Before handoff, compare the saved packet with the latest scoped requirements and verify the requested form or authentic original is included without disturbing unrelated records.

Classify each input as source/user-confirmed, assistant-selected unconfirmed, external fact unavailable, controlled action/evidence, or genuinely inapplicable. Fill all source-backed and user-confirmed writable inputs proactively. Under the active instruction, make reasonable task-consistent drafting choices for remaining author-controlled planning or narrative inputs and mark their exact unconfirmed characters red. Do not seek repeated permission for those choices or leave them empty merely because the document is a draft. A calculation depending on an unconfirmed choice retains that uncertainty; correct multiplication does not turn an assumed input into a verified fact.

Red does not permit invented external facts: identities, contact or account details, registrations, actual quotes, prescribed document numbers, actual dates, attendance, results, payment or approval must have evidence. Never fabricate a signature, seal, consent, inquiry result, accomplishment, payment or approval, even in red. Preserve authentic supplied material and existing user entries within their authorized use. If an essential field or attachment requires an unavailable external fact or another person's action, keep that specific input unasserted and report the concrete remaining dependency briefly outside the document; continue everything else that can be completed.

When the user limits content to supplied materials and states that absent information does not exist, treat that as a scope decision: do not invent missing survey results or ask for absent data merely to complete a template. Use supported content and omit inapplicable claims; request clarification only if an actual unresolved requirement cannot otherwise be satisfied.

When a document or poster includes a QR code for a demo and the user specifies its hosting route, verify that the demo is actually deployed through that named route; do not reuse an earlier provider's artifact or URL. Encode only the successfully deployed, verified target URL. Decode the QR from the exact final saved artifact and compare it with that URL, then report the verified access audience. Do not silently publish or broaden access; if deployment, target verification, or authorized audience cannot be established, leave the QR unasserted and report the blocker.

Printed approval/cooperation roles or example routing in an official form or prior document establish the form or precedent, not the current activity's mandatory approval route. Independently verify relevance and applicability from current instructions or the responsible office before classifying a role as confirmed. If the user expressly authorizes proposed unconfirmed routing roles and has selected red marking, mark only those proposed role characters within that scope; do not recolor or delete fixed form labels by default. Keep unsigned signature fields blank and never imply actual approval or invent a signature. Report unresolved routing for office confirmation outside the form.

For participant fields, prepare an independent population map per event/date, document purpose and expense before copying names or totals. Record included and excluded people or roles, the latest user/source basis, planned versus actual stage, expected count and each saved destination. Programme attendance, sign-in recipients, meal/admission eligibility, intended insurance recipients, paid teaching roles, learners and actual attendance at a separate activity are distinct populations. Inclusion in one does not authorize inclusion in another; an insurance list does not prove enrollment. On correction, compare the old and new maps and update only affected roles, fields and dependent totals; never globally replace headcounts or generalize one person's exclusion into a role-wide rule. At handoff, reconcile every saved row, exclusion and repeated total with its own map and preserve unaffected populations.

When a correction rejects an agent-added explanation, make a private preflight map of the rejected meaning and exact observed variants, all current in-scope artifacts and their attachment lists, and protected official wording or source values. At final handoff inspect every saved artifact and list for the rejected explanation and paraphrases, including places not edited in the last pass. Preserve required template parentheses, legal wording, and source-backed values; never strip parentheses globally. Distinguish an optional attachment role that current evidence and the user exclude, which is omitted entirely when permitted, from a mandatory question whose official inapplicable choice must be marked when supported. Do not add an explanatory substitute. Resolve an actual template or requirement conflict separately.

## Substantive detail in requested content fields

For poster redesigns, independently inventory the complete source-supported baseline information before selecting content or arranging variants. When the user requests a quieter, less cluttered result, establish a clear reading hierarchy and reduce unnecessary visual groups and competing accents while preserving any user-selected fixed background. If both compressed and full-information directions are requested, select the essentials deliberately for the compressed direction and check the full direction for substantive coverage against the independent baseline; do not treat a longer-looking summary as complete. Keep each requested model's work independently attributable and preserve the requested number and density of variants; do not imply a model produced a variant without evidence. Compare the actual rendered variants for meaningful differences in composition, hierarchy and information treatment, not just palette, while respecting the user's fixed elements. Do not impose arbitrary word counts, fonts or a fixed concept.

When pictures are explicitly requested or a poster redesign follows an image-led reference, preserve informative image coverage unless the user authorizes a different direction. A quieter or simpler composition must not be interpreted as text-only: simplify grouping and competing accents without deleting meaningful illustrations. Before composing variants, map the source-backed section messages to their image or diagram roles and intended body locations, distinguishing informative contextual pictures from decoration. Icons, arrows and colored boxes alone do not substitute for contextual pictures. Choose visual treatment freely within the user's format and protected elements; do not impose arbitrary image counts, area quotas or one picture per section. Generated illustrations may explain supported concepts, but must not introduce unsupported hard facts or pose as photographs, measured results or authentic evidence.

Inspect informative image crops for the visible subject and explanatory action, not just aspect-ratio fit. Preserve the people, meaningful props and activity needed to understand the scene; reject unintended sliced-person or furniture fragments. Review the crop as actually placed at the intended poster scale, allowing deliberate close-ups that retain the intended meaning.

Reopen and render each exact saved poster variant, then compare its body with that visual plan and the reference's informative coverage. Verify that the planned pictures or diagrams are actually present, legible and communicate their associated messages. For source visuals that must be retained, resolve the active slide-picture relationships to the intended media and check its source identity/hash; an unused image still inside the ZIP is not visual retention. A template header, logo, QR code, decorative glyphs, embedded-media count or earlier preview cannot establish body-picture coverage. If the body remains predominantly text where informative pictures were requested, repair the visual composition before claiming completion; report a concrete unavailable-source or rendering blocker rather than treating decorative substitutes as sufficient.

Calibrate poster typography to the physical page dimensions, supplied reference and intended reading distance, then review legibility at a scale representative of the final output. Do not impose a universal point-size minimum or shrink substantive content merely to fit. For PPTX posters, inspect the exact final saved file in native PowerPoint: check the preview and native text bounds for clipping and collisions, especially adjacent comparison rows, inner borders and the QR quiet area. An approximate renderer alone cannot establish native fit. Bind the preview and fit-check result to that file's SHA-256 and recheck after a save changes it; an earlier preview cannot clear a later file. If native verification is unavailable, report the specific unverified step rather than claiming it passed.

An existing PowerPoint process alone is not an authorization blocker for native verification. When authorized verification can safely attach to the shared application, open only the target deck read-only (`ReadOnly=True`, `WithWindow=False`), export it, and close only presentations opened by the current verification. Preserve pre-existing presentations and the user's active presentation/view, compare their before/after state, and never call `Quit` on the shared application. Record the actual rendering engine accurately. Missing Korean glyphs or font-subsetting failures in a fallback renderer do not establish source corruption; check the exact file in native PowerPoint before attributing the defect to the source or changing it as a repair.

When a form asks for detailed educational or activity content, inspect the current plan and available teaching or activity materials independently of the candidate. Map each applicable time and place to the educational topic, concrete participant activity and any planned output the form actually requires, with exact source locations and target fields. Write enough source-backed detail to explain what happens in each activity; generic activity labels, repeated goals or a nonempty cell do not establish completeness when richer relevant evidence is available. A detailed separate attachment does not replace the main field's requested content unless the form or user permits that reference.

When the user asks to expand existing narrative cells, first map the source's key content, activity sequence, methods and applicable uses independently of the old wording and page layout. Assign supported points to each field's actual purpose: for example, major content can explain what is taught and how, while operating results can describe supported content organized or review criteria, only to the extent the source establishes what occurred. Compare each complete saved cell with its pre-edit text and the independent map: require a meaningful increase in explanation for each requested cell, not merely longer wording, keyword lists, duplicated points or a detail moved to an attachment. Check the exact saved text and a fresh render for readability and overflow. A narrow cell or assumed one-page layout must not drive the answer back to a short summary or force font shrinking; accommodate source-backed detail within the form's authorized layout. If the user explicitly requires one page, preserve that requirement and report a genuine content/legibility conflict rather than claiming completion. Do not assert unsupported actions or outcomes, even in red.

For a user-corrected session or round label, independently map the exact canonical wording, event identity and authorized current-field scope before editing. Distinguish the round label from frequency and quantity, and verify each affected saved occurrence against that map. Do not globally replace a shared word or alter other periods, originals or calendar entries merely because they contain a similar label.

For requested instructional evidence, independently map each actual target slot to its required category and current authorized source: an activity photograph and a teaching-material image are separate roles. Before placing images from a user-supplied layout, apply the reference-defined group-count and main-page disposition gate in [the evidence-photo layout reference](../hancom-hwpx-documents/references/procurement-forms.md#consistent-evidence-photo-layout). Pass the independently specified counts, rows/columns, table structure, same-page grouping and density to the authoring invocation; readable representatives or a complete appendix cannot replace a requested main-page grid. When slides are requested for the materials slot, inspect the actual current slide content and insert legible images of the relevant slides; a classroom photograph showing a projector is not a substitute. At final saved-file rendering, verify each slot, image identity, topic and readability, as well as all required source-supported content in the main educational field. Image presence, a generic one-line field, an earlier render or a detailed attachment alone cannot pass these checks. If no eligible source or legible reproduction exists, report the specific unresolved slot rather than inventing evidence.

Classify spare, structural and genuinely unused rows separately from omitted activities. Preserve fixed rows; remove or consolidate an unnecessary row only when the form and authorized scope permit it. Do not invent activities, schedule slots, paid teaching hours or outcomes to occupy space. Judge sufficiency by the field's purpose and independent source mapping, not an arbitrary minimum character count. For meeting minutes, independently map the agenda and available supporting discussion or decisions to the actual minutes field; include supported substantive points and follow-up responsibilities or actions when evidenced and applicable. A generic one-line outcome does not cover available relevant details. A plan establishes planned topics, not proof of discussion, a decision, attendance or an outcome; leave unsupported actual facts unasserted.

## Exact color and format preservation

An explicit current request or a standing user preference to mark choices red is sufficient authorization; do not ask the same user again on every document. In public/shared use, activate this assumption-marking mode only when the user has selected it, currently or as a standing instruction. Without that selection, do not silently invent an assumption-marking convention.

Keep the template's and user's existing normal colors, including blue or other fixed colors. Confirmed inputs retain their applicable normal formatting; do not normalize them all to black. Color only the exact characters of still-unconfirmed assistant choices. Preserve labels, text, fonts, styles, tables, formulas, field roles, approvals and source facts. Later prose or grammar passes must preserve the provenance-to-span mapping and authorized colors; recheck affected spans after an authorized text revision.

For HWPX, use [the existing red-span gate](../hancom-hwpx-documents/references/red-assumption-spans.md): independently map provenance before entering/coloring, verify the filled pre-color candidate, preserve and hash it, then verify the final exact-character colors and unchanged text/structure. Cite the standing instruction in `red_instruction` with `red_instruction_scope: "standing_preference"` when applicable. For DOCX and spreadsheets, use an equivalent source-bound run/rich-text or cell check and current render; do not color a whole cell when only part is an assumption. If the format/tool cannot preserve the required span, report that concrete limitation. A color, text, source mapping or preservation failure is `VERIFICATION_FAILED`: roll back the affected candidate to its verified baseline and repair it before delivery; never pass it by broadening the allowed color changes.

## Completion and handoff

Reconcile the saved output list and destinations with the pre-write programme-to-folder grouping map and any later explicit user change. Verify each document and attachment remains in its intended packet, with no unintended combined packet, split or duplicate; report a specific unresolved boundary rather than claiming grouping is verified. A correct document count alone does not establish correct grouping.

Reopen the exact saved artifacts and reconcile every inventoried input, attachment, repeated value, quantity, financial role and applicable total with its source and provenance. For requested detailed content, compare the complete saved cells or paragraphs with the independent topic-and-activity map above, including supported associations and required planned outputs; field-presence or exact-text checks alone do not establish substantive sufficiency. Inspect current rendering for readable detail, clipping and overflow and use the format's normal structural/openability checks. Each blank must have a specific justified disposition, not a generic draft excuse. A color check alone does not verify factual truth, field coverage, attachment completeness or submission eligibility.

Do not insert unrequested `초안`, `미정`, `추후 입력`, `확인 필요` or process commentary into the form. Preserve mandatory template wording and user-entered status text. When the current output is complete, replace only agent-generated draft filename labels with a clear canonical business-role name; preserve an explicit user-chosen filename, avoid collisions and update in-scope references if a rename is actually needed. This is not permission to bulk-rename history, source forms or other workers' files.

Create no duplicate PDF, review bundle or status sidecar unless requested or required for confirmed submission/printing; temporary verification outputs stay outside active records. Report prepared contents, red decisions and any real remaining blocker accurately. Complete preparation is distinct from actual signing, consent, approval, sending, submission and payment; do not claim those actions occurred from a finished file.
