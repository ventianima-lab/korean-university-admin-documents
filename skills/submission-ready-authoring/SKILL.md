# Complete document authoring

When a user selects complete-for-submission preparation with red marking of unconfirmed assistant choices, honor that current request or standing preference across its applicable document-writing tasks without repeated permission questions. A later explicit task restriction controls; do not assume every user's opt-in.

For requested document artifacts, produce the complete usable document or packet within the authorized scope. Calling a working file a draft does not justify leaving writable fields or required attachments unfinished. Respect a deliberate outline-only, review-only or limited-field request; this policy does not authorize changing unrelated records.

## Fill from an independent inventory

Find and inspect the current official or user-designated form, instructions, current source records and applicable precedents. Use the latest working final for follow-ups. Before entry, independently enumerate all real inputs and required attachments, including repeated fields, and map exact source locations and output coordinates. Keep fixed labels, decorative blanks and signer-controlled areas outside ordinary fill scope. A list copied from the candidate cannot establish completeness. Enumerate by document and page or region, including metadata inputs on photo-evidence sheets as well as picture slots; for each repeated session map its date and location separately to the governing source. Confirm every authorized form and region was reviewed before a selected-scope check can support packet completion. Preserve decorative blanks, signature and approval areas, and distinguish planned dates from actual attendance.

For physical receipts, inventory the receipt evidence and any attachment form as separate roles: map who obtains and attaches the original, and who prepares the form, using the current instructions and applicable official template. A user's decision to attach originals personally or omit receipt images does not exclude an applicable or requested attachment form. Respect an explicit exclusion of the form; do not require one universally. Before handoff, reconcile the saved packet against this role inventory and visually verify any requested physical mounting space is present and empty. Do not fabricate or print a receipt in that space.

Likewise distinguish user-controlled signing or attendance from preparation of an applicable or requested meeting sign-in form. Track each meeting's form, existing signed original, preparation responsibility, and inclusion or exclusion separately; a later explicit inclusion supersedes an earlier exclusion for its stated scope only. Do not require a new sign-in form for every event. Prefer the existing authentic signed original when applicable and preserve it unchanged; use authorized identity sources for any prepared form, leave unsupported signatures blank, and never infer attendance from a printed name. Before handoff, compare the saved packet with the latest scoped requirements and verify the requested form or authentic original is included without disturbing unrelated records.

Classify each input as source/user-confirmed, assistant-selected unconfirmed, external fact unavailable, controlled action/evidence, or genuinely inapplicable. Fill all source-backed and user-confirmed writable inputs proactively. Under the active instruction, make reasonable task-consistent drafting choices for remaining author-controlled planning or narrative inputs and mark their exact unconfirmed characters red. Do not seek repeated permission for those choices or leave them empty merely because the document is a draft. A calculation depending on an unconfirmed choice retains that uncertainty; correct multiplication does not turn an assumed input into a verified fact.

Red does not permit invented external facts: identities, contact or account details, registrations, actual quotes, prescribed document numbers, actual dates, attendance, results, payment or approval must have evidence. Never fabricate a signature, seal, consent, inquiry result, accomplishment, payment or approval, even in red. Preserve authentic supplied material and existing user entries within their authorized use. If an essential field or attachment requires an unavailable external fact or another person's action, keep that specific input unasserted and report the concrete remaining dependency briefly outside the document; continue everything else that can be completed.

Printed approval/cooperation roles or example routing in an official form or prior document establish the form or precedent, not the current activity's mandatory approval route. Independently verify relevance and applicability from current instructions or the responsible office before classifying a role as confirmed. If the user expressly authorizes proposed unconfirmed routing roles and has selected red marking, mark only those proposed role characters within that scope; do not recolor or delete fixed form labels by default. Keep unsigned signature fields blank and never imply actual approval or invent a signature. Report unresolved routing for office confirmation outside the form.

For participant fields, prepare an independent population map per event/date, document purpose and expense before copying names or totals. Record included and excluded people or roles, the latest user/source basis, planned versus actual stage, expected count and each saved destination. Programme attendance, sign-in recipients, meal/admission eligibility, intended insurance recipients, paid teaching roles, learners and actual attendance at a separate activity are distinct populations. Inclusion in one does not authorize inclusion in another; an insurance list does not prove enrollment. On correction, compare the old and new maps and update only affected roles, fields and dependent totals; never globally replace headcounts or generalize one person's exclusion into a role-wide rule. At handoff, reconcile every saved row, exclusion and repeated total with its own map and preserve unaffected populations.

## Substantive detail in requested content fields

When a form asks for detailed educational or activity content, inspect the current plan and available teaching or activity materials independently of the candidate. Map each applicable time and place to the educational topic, concrete participant activity and any planned output the form actually requires, with exact source locations and target fields. Write enough source-backed detail to explain what happens in each activity; generic activity labels, repeated goals or a nonempty cell do not establish completeness when richer relevant evidence is available. A detailed separate attachment does not replace the main field's requested content unless the form or user permits that reference.

Classify spare, structural and genuinely unused rows separately from omitted activities. Preserve fixed rows; remove or consolidate an unnecessary row only when the form and authorized scope permit it. Do not invent activities, schedule slots, paid teaching hours or outcomes to occupy space. Judge sufficiency by the field's purpose and independent source mapping, not an arbitrary minimum character count. For meeting minutes, independently map the agenda and available supporting discussion or decisions to the actual minutes field; include supported substantive points and follow-up responsibilities or actions when evidenced and applicable. A generic one-line outcome does not cover available relevant details. A plan establishes planned topics, not proof of discussion, a decision, attendance or an outcome; leave unsupported actual facts unasserted.

## Exact color and format preservation

An explicit current request or a standing user preference to mark choices red is sufficient authorization; do not ask the same user again on every document. In public/shared use, activate this assumption-marking mode only when the user has selected it, currently or as a standing instruction. Without that selection, do not silently invent an assumption-marking convention.

Keep the template's and user's existing normal colors, including blue or other fixed colors. Confirmed inputs retain their applicable normal formatting; do not normalize them all to black. Color only the exact characters of still-unconfirmed assistant choices. Preserve labels, text, fonts, styles, tables, formulas, field roles, approvals and source facts. Later prose or grammar passes must preserve the provenance-to-span mapping and authorized colors; recheck affected spans after an authorized text revision.

For HWPX, use [the existing red-span gate](../hancom-hwpx-documents/references/red-assumption-spans.md): independently map provenance before entering/coloring, verify the filled pre-color candidate, preserve and hash it, then verify the final exact-character colors and unchanged text/structure. Cite the standing instruction in `red_instruction` with `red_instruction_scope: "standing_preference"` when applicable. For DOCX and spreadsheets, use an equivalent source-bound run/rich-text or cell check and current render; do not color a whole cell when only part is an assumption. If the format/tool cannot preserve the required span, report that concrete limitation. A color, text, source mapping or preservation failure is `VERIFICATION_FAILED`: roll back the affected candidate to its verified baseline and repair it before delivery; never pass it by broadening the allowed color changes.

## Completion and handoff

Reopen the exact saved artifacts and reconcile every inventoried input, attachment, repeated value, quantity, financial role and applicable total with its source and provenance. For requested detailed content, compare the complete saved cells or paragraphs with the independent topic-and-activity map above, including supported associations and required planned outputs; field-presence or exact-text checks alone do not establish substantive sufficiency. Inspect current rendering for readable detail, clipping and overflow and use the format's normal structural/openability checks. Each blank must have a specific justified disposition, not a generic draft excuse. A color check alone does not verify factual truth, field coverage, attachment completeness or submission eligibility.

Do not insert unrequested `초안`, `미정`, `추후 입력`, `확인 필요` or process commentary into the form. Preserve mandatory template wording and user-entered status text. When the current output is complete, replace only agent-generated draft filename labels with a clear canonical business-role name; preserve an explicit user-chosen filename, avoid collisions and update in-scope references if a rename is actually needed. This is not permission to bulk-rename history, source forms or other workers' files.

Create no duplicate PDF, review bundle or status sidecar unless requested or required for confirmed submission/printing; temporary verification outputs stay outside active records. Report prepared contents, red decisions and any real remaining blocker accurately. Complete preparation is distinct from actual signing, consent, approval, sending, submission and payment; do not claim those actions occurred from a finished file.
