# University administrative document types

## Official letter

Confirm sender, recipient, title, grounds, requested action, deadline, attachments, document number and issue date. Treat the letter as the cover for a business packet; do not invent an approval state.

## Plan

Confirm purpose, basis, period, place, participants, responsibilities, detailed schedule, budget source, calculation basis, safety or privacy requirements, expected outputs, approval route and attachments.

## Result report

Keep planned and actual values distinct. Confirm actual dates, attendance, expenses, outcomes, evidence, exceptions and follow-up. Do not copy expected outcomes into the results section as completed facts.

## Budget and purchase packet

Separate plan evidence, approved budget, quotation, comparison quotation, vendor evidence, order, delivery, inspection, receipt and payment records. A past vendor or price is not a new contract fact.

## Roster and attendance form

Preserve row order, identity associations, signature columns and privacy. Confirm that all rows fit, headers repeat when needed, and signature cells remain usable.

## Notice or message

Lead with the action and deadline, give the exact route or link, separate exceptions, and retain official terminology. Do not expose internal notes.
